Nyang Jelly Pop Privacy Policy

  • Operator: MORAON Studio · Representative: Sungbin Moon · Game brand: SOBOK Games
  • Business registration number ( Republic of Korea ): 523-43-01343
  • Privacy officer and contact: Sungbin Moon · sobokgames@gmail.com
  • App: Nyang Jelly Pop ( Korean title: 냥글젤디 - 냥젤리팝 ) — Android package · iOS bundle ID: com.sobokgames.nyanglejellypop
  • Effective date: November 11, 2026

MORAON Studio ( game brand SOBOK Games, “we” ) values the privacy of our players. This policy explains how information is handled in the app above, including test versions. If the Korean version and this translation differ, the Korean version prevails.

1. Information we collect directly

We do not run our own servers and do not directly collect personal information such as your name or contact details. However, if you choose to sign in with an account ( Google or Apple ) in Settings, your account information and a copy of your game progress are stored in a Firebase project we manage ( section 6 ). The app's ads ( section 2 ), in-app purchases ( section 3 ) and Google Play Games ( section 4 ) use Google's services, and Google processes the information described below. On iPhone, purchases, Game Center and iCloud saving use Apple's services ( section 5 ).

2. Ads ( Google AdMob )

  • The app shows rewarded ads only when you tap an ad button to receive a reward. Ads are provided by Google AdMob ( Google Mobile Ads SDK ) of Google LLC.
  • When the app starts, it prepares ads and checks whether ad consent is required. When ads are loaded and shown, Google automatically collects:
    • Device identifiers: Android advertising ID, app set ID, etc.
    • IP address ( may be used to estimate approximate location )
    • App usage such as app launches, taps and ad video views
    • Performance and diagnostic information of the app and the ads SDK, such as app launch time
  • This information is shared with Google for ad delivery and measurement, analytics and fraud prevention, and is encrypted in transit ( TLS ). Google's processing is governed by the Google Privacy Policy and How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.
  • The app requests only ads rated for parental guidance ( PG ) or lower.
  • In regions where consent is required, such as the European Economic Area ( EEA ), the United Kingdom and Switzerland, the app asks for ad consent with Google's consent management tool when you first open it, and you can change your choice at any time with the “Ad privacy settings” button in Settings.
  • You can reset or delete your advertising ID in your Android device settings ( usually “Privacy › Ads” or “Google › Ads” ).
  • If you buy “Remove Ads”, you receive rewards instantly without watching ads.

3. In-app purchases ( Google Play billing )

  • Paid items in the app ( Remove Ads, Starter Pack, coins ) are sold through Google Play billing. Payment information such as payment method, billing and receipts is processed directly by Google; we do not receive or store card numbers or other payment information.
  • To deliver purchased items and avoid delivering the same purchase twice, the app stores the purchased items and purchase confirmation data ( purchase tokens ) received from Google Play only on your device. This information is not sent to us.
  • Payments and refunds are governed by Google Play's terms and refund policy and the Google Privacy Policy.

4. Google Play Games ( leaderboards · achievements · cloud save )

  • The app provides leaderboards, achievements and cloud saves ( saved games ) through Google Play Games services of Google LLC. If your device has a Play Games profile, you are signed in automatically when the app starts, or you can sign in with the “Sign in to Google Play Games” button in Settings. You can play without signing in.
  • When you sign in, Google processes:
    • Play Games profile: player ID, gamer name, avatar
    • Your best scores ( leaderboards ) and unlocked achievements in this game
    • Cloud save: a copy of your game progress to continue across devices ( the same content as the on-device data in section 7: scores and records, coins and items, rank, Jellydex · decoration · mission · check-in status, delivery records of purchased items, etc. )
    • Analytics and diagnostics to improve the stability and quality of the Play Games SDK
  • This information is stored on Google's servers and encrypted in transit ( HTTPS ). Who can see your leaderboard and achievement activity ( everyone · friends · only you ) follows your Play Games profile settings. We can see leaderboard and achievement statistics in the Play Games console, but we do not receive your contact details or Google account information.
  • You can delete this game's data or your whole Play Games account from your Play Games profile, and manage sign-in in Android settings or the Play Games app. Google's processing is governed by the Google Privacy Policy.

5. iPhone app ( App Tracking Transparency · App Store purchases · Game Center · iCloud save )

On iPhone, ads are provided by Google AdMob as in section 2, and purchases, leaderboards, achievements and cloud saves are provided by Apple Inc. The following applies instead of sections 3 and 4.

  • App Tracking Transparency: When you first open the app, iOS asks whether to allow use of the advertising identifier ( IDFA ). Google uses the IDFA for personalized ads and ad measurement only if you allow it; the game and ad rewards work the same if you don't. You can change this at any time in iOS Settings › Privacy & Security › Tracking. Regardless of your choice, the IP address, app usage, performance and diagnostic information in section 2 and the identifier for vendors ( IDFV ) may be used for ad delivery and fraud prevention.
  • App Store purchases: Paid items ( Remove Ads, Starter Pack, coins ) are sold through Apple's App Store. Payment and billing information is processed directly by Apple; to deliver items and avoid delivering the same purchase twice, the app stores only the purchased items and transaction IDs on your device. You can get Remove Ads back on the same Apple account with the “Restore purchases” button in the shop. Payments and refunds are governed by the App Store terms and the Apple Privacy Policy.
  • Game Center: If your device is signed in to Game Center, you are signed in automatically when the app starts, or you can sign in with the button in Settings. You can play without signing in. When signed in, Apple processes your Game Center player ID and nickname, best scores ( leaderboards ) and unlocked achievements; visibility follows your Game Center settings. You can manage sign-in and visibility in iOS Settings › Game Center.
  • iCloud save: While signed in to Game Center, a copy of your game progress ( the same content as section 7 ) is saved to your own iCloud ( key-value storage ) to continue across devices. Only devices on the same Apple account can read it, and we cannot see it. If you turn off iCloud in iOS Settings, this device stops saving and syncing.
  • Information processed by Apple is governed by the Apple Privacy Policy.

6. Account sign-in ( Google · Apple )

If you sign in with a Google or Apple account in the “Account” section of Settings, you can continue the same progress on other devices ( including between Android and iPhone ). Signing in is optional; you can play without it. Sign-in is provided by Firebase ( Firebase Authentication, Cloud Firestore ) of Google LLC.

  • Information processed: Firebase user ID, the email address of the signed-in account ( if you choose “Hide My Email” with Apple, the relay address created by Apple ), sign-in method ( Google or Apple ), account creation and last sign-in time, a copy of your game progress ( the same content as section 7 ), and a random value identifying the device that saved last
  • Purpose: verifying sign-in and continuing progress across devices. It is not used for ads or marketing.
  • Storage and protection: stored in a Firebase project we manage. Game progress copies are stored in Cloud Firestore in the Seoul ( Republic of Korea ) data center, and account information on Google's Firebase Authentication servers. Security rules allow you to read and write only your own progress, and data is encrypted in transit ( HTTPS ).
  • Retention and deletion: kept until you delete your account. Tapping “Delete account” in Settings immediately deletes your account and the cloud copy of your progress ( for Apple sign-in, the link to the app is also revoked ); progress on your device remains. You can also request deletion by email after uninstalling the app.
  • While signed in, your progress copy is saved to your account instead of the Play Games cloud save in section 4 ( iCloud in section 5 on iPhone ). Leaderboards and achievements still use Play Games ( Game Center on iPhone ).

How to delete your Nyang Jelly Pop account

  1. In the app, open Settings ( gear icon ) › “Account” and tap “Delete account”.
  2. Confirm deletion, then sign in once more with the Google or Apple account you used to verify it's you.
  3. Your account and the progress saved to it are deleted immediately.

If you've already uninstalled the app or can't delete it in the app, email sobokgames@gmail.com with the email address you signed in with and ask for “Nyang Jelly Pop account deletion”. We will delete it within 7 days after verifying your identity and let you know.

Deleted information: Firebase user ID, email address, sign-in method, account creation and sign-in times, and the game progress copy and device value saved to your account. Nothing is kept separately. Progress on your device, Play Games ( Game Center · iCloud on iPhone ) records and payment records are separate from your account and remain; you can delete them as described in sections 4, 5 and 7.

7. Game information stored on your device

Game progress, scores and records, coins and items, Jellydex · decoration · mission · check-in status, delivery records of purchased items and app settings are stored on your device to provide the game and are not sent to us. Deleting the app or clearing its data may delete this information. However, a “Remove Ads” purchase is restored when you reinstall the app with the same Google account, and on iPhone you can get it back with “Restore purchases” on the same Apple account. “Backup · Restore” in Settings turns your game data into a backup code that you keep yourself; this code is not sent to us either. If your device or operating system backup is turned on, backup and restore follow those settings. If you sign in to Google Play Games ( Game Center on iPhone ), a copy with the same content is also kept in the cloud save ( iCloud on iPhone ) as in section 4 ( section 5 ); if you sign in to an account, it is kept in your account as in section 6. Before switching to progress from another device, this device's progress is kept separately on the device.

8. App permissions and network

The app uses internet and network state permissions to load ads, and the ads SDK includes the advertising ID permission ( com.google.android.gms.permission.AD_ID ) and the Android Privacy Sandbox ad services permission. It uses the Google Play billing permission ( com.android.vending.BILLING ) for in-app purchases; Google Play Games works through Google Play services without additional device permissions. The app does not request location, contacts, photos, camera or microphone permissions. On iPhone, the app asks for “App Tracking Transparency” ( section 5 ), and asks for add-only access to the Photos app only when you save a Jelly Room photo with “Save Image” in the share sheet ( it does not read existing photos ). Game images and sounds are files included in the app.

9. Sharing with third parties and international transfers

We do not provide your information to third parties. We entrust the storage and processing of account sign-in information to Google LLC ( Firebase ), and information is transferred abroad through Google's ads, billing, Play Games and Firebase features and ( on iPhone ) Apple's purchase, Game Center and iCloud features, as follows.

  • Recipients: Google LLC ( contact: see the Google Privacy Policy ); on iPhone, Apple Inc. ( contact: see the Apple Privacy Policy )
  • Countries: the United States and other countries where Google or Apple data centers are located ( game progress copies for account sign-in are stored in the Seoul, Republic of Korea data center )
  • When and how: sent over the network when the app prepares ads at startup, loads and shows ads, processes payments, uploads scores · achievements or saves progress to the cloud ( iCloud on iPhone, or your account when signed in ) while signed in to Google Play Games ( Game Center on iPhone ) — at the end of each round, once a minute and when you leave the app — and when you sign in to an account
  • Items: ad-related information in section 2, payment information processed directly by Google in section 3, Play Games information in section 4, purchase · Game Center · iCloud information processed by Apple in section 5, and account sign-in information in section 6
  • Purposes: ad delivery and measurement, analytics, fraud prevention, payment processing, account sign-in, leaderboards · achievements and continuing progress across devices
  • Retention: as set out in the Google Privacy Policy ( Play Games information until you delete it from your Play Games profile ); information processed by Apple as set out in the Apple Privacy Policy; account sign-in information until you delete your account
  • How to refuse: ads are not shown unless you tap an ad button, and you can delete your advertising ID in device settings. Play Games · Game Center · account sign-in information is not sent unless you sign in; account sign-in information can be deleted with “Delete account” in the app, and Play Games information from your Play Games profile. On iPhone, ads are served without the IDFA if you deny “App Tracking Transparency”. If you do not want even the transfers that happen when the app prepares ads at startup, you can stop using and uninstall the app.

10. Children and intended audience

The app's target age on Google Play is 13 and over, and the iPhone app follows the same standard. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. Parents or guardians with privacy questions about the app can contact us below.

11. Your rights and contact

If you have not signed in to an account, we do not hold any of your information. Account sign-in information and the progress saved to your account can be deleted immediately with “Delete account” in Settings, and you can request access, correction, deletion or suspension of processing at the contact below. Game information on your device can be deleted by clearing app data or uninstalling the app; ad-related information can be managed by deleting or resetting your advertising ID in device settings and ( where applicable ) with “Ad privacy settings” in the app; leaderboard · achievement · cloud save information can be managed and deleted in your Play Games profile. On iPhone, manage these in iOS Settings under Tracking, Game Center and iCloud. For information processed by Google and Apple, exercise your rights as described in each company's privacy policy. Send privacy questions or requests to sobokgames@gmail.com.
