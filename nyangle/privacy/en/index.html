Nyangle Jelly ( the "App" ) treats your personal information with care and follows applicable laws, including Korea's Personal Information Protection Act. This policy explains what information the App processes, where, and why.

1. Information the App processes

If you don't sign in ( guest ), no information is sent to the operator. Game progress ( levels · stars · coins · collected cats · decorations · league points · nickname · settings ) and purchase records are stored on your device. Other league participants are virtual opponents created by the App. The App does not read your location, contacts, microphone, or camera.

If you choose to link an account, the App processes the following.

  • The App never receives your password. Sign-in happens on Google's or Apple's sign-in screen, and the App only receives confirmation that you signed in.
  • If you email us, your address and message are used only to reply and are deleted once the inquiry is resolved.

2. Advertising ( Google AdMob )

To stay free, the App shows Google AdMob ads ( rewarded ads · interstitial ads ). For advertising, Google's ad SDK may process the following.

ItemsPurpose

IP address ( including approximate location )Serving ads · fraud prevention
Ad interactions ( views · taps ), error / diagnostic informationAd measurement · quality improvement · fraud prevention
  • In regions that require consent ( EEA · UK · Switzerland, etc. ), a consent form is shown before ads load, and you can change your choice anytime under Settings → Ad privacy settings in the App.
  • In your device settings ( Settings → Privacy / Google → Ads ), you can reset or delete your advertising ID and turn off personalized ads.
  • Information processed by Google is governed by the Google Privacy Policy and How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.
  • If you buy "Remove Ads", interstitial ads no longer appear ( rewarded ads that you choose to watch remain available ).

5. Children

The App is intended for users aged 13 and older. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If a parent or guardian learns that a child's information was processed, please contact us.

6. Retention and deletion

  • Game records on the device are deleted immediately when you uninstall the App or go to "Settings → Apps → Nyangle Jelly → Storage → Clear data". The App is set to exclude game data from device backup / device-to-device transfer.
  • A signed-in account and its cloud save can be deleted anytime under Settings → Account sign-in → "Delete account" in the App. If you can't use the App, email us ( we delete it without delay after verification ).
  • Advertising, payment, and game service information follows each company's ( Google · Apple · Samsung · ONE store ) retention rules, and you can request deletion through that company's process.

8. Your rights

You can request access to, correction, deletion, or suspension of processing of your personal information at any time. Delete the account and cloud save yourself with "Delete account" ( section 6 ), delete on-device records by clearing the App's data, and contact the relevant company for advertising, payment, and game service information. If you need help, please contact us.
